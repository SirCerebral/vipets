The fine print

Shipping, returns & live animal policies

Shipping

Flat $7.99 for dry goods anywhere in the lower 48. Free over $75. Most orders ship in 1–2 business days.

In-store pickup

Free pickup at any location, ready in 2 hours during open hours. We'll text you when it's ready.

Live animals

Live fish, reptiles, rabbits and puppies are reserve & pickup only. We never ship live animals. Every live animal includes our standard health guarantee.

Returns

Unopened dry goods returnable within 30 days with receipt. Food is non-returnable once opened — call us and we'll work it out.

VIP Card

Free to join. 10% off most pet supplies and dog & cat food, every day. Excludes feeder fish, feeder rodents, crickets, live plants and gift cards. Never expires.
